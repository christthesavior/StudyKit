Home/Privacy

Privacy Policy

StudyKit processes your files and text inside your browser for every tool listed on this site: PDF → Text, Image → Text (OCR), PDF Merge, PDF Compressor, Image → PDF, Word Counter, Case Converter, Text Summarizer, Percentage Calculator, GPA Calculator and Study Timer.

What we never do

No file you open here is uploaded to a server. There is no account system and we do not collect names, emails or documents.

Third-party code

PDF and OCR engines are downloaded from public CDNs the first time you use those tools; your browser may then cache them. Those CDNs can see a plain request for the library file — not your documents.

Stored on your device

Only your theme preference, kept in your browser's localStorage. Clear your site data and it is gone.

Home/About

About StudyKit

StudyKit started as a folder of small scripts used the night before deadlines: count the words, pull text out of a lecture PDF, work out whether that C+ actually sinks the semester.

Everything here runs client-side, so it is fast, it works on a weak connection, and your coursework stays yours. No sign-up wall, no watermarked output, no “upgrade to download”.
